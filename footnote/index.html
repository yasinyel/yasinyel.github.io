Footnote

Study material from your lecture PDFs

Footnote turns lecture PDFs into flashcards, multiple-choice questions and study notes. Every card stays linked to the line it came from. For Mac, iPhone and iPad.

  • Made on your device; your PDFs are never sent to a server.
  • Spaced repetition that asks just before you would forget.
  • Worksheets with an answer key, for teachers.
  • In sync between your Mac and iPhone through iCloud.
  • No account, no subscription.
