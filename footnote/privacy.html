Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Footnote app for Mac, iPhone and iPad (the "App") handles your data.

1. Who we are

Yasin Yel — yasinyel32@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

The App collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The App contains no third-party analytics or advertising library.

3. Your cards, PDFs and study history

Your decks, cards, review history, study notes and the PDFs you add are stored on your device.

4. iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your decks, cards and review history are kept in sync between your devices through the private database of your own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit). We cannot read that data. PDF files are not synced; they stay on the device they were added on. You can delete the App's iCloud data in Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage Storage.

5. Generating cards, questions and notes

Cards, multiple-choice questions and study notes are generated from your PDF by the Apple Intelligence model on your device. The content of your documents is not sent to us or to any server.

6. Importing and exporting

Deck files, CSV files and backups are read from and written to the places you choose. They never reach us.

7. Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification created on your device, only if you turn it on. No marketing notifications are sent.

8. Purchases

Footnote Pro is a one-time purchase processed by the Apple App Store. Your payment details never reach us.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children. Because no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We hold no data about you. You can remove everything by deleting the App and, if you used sync, its iCloud data. For any question, write to yasinyel32@gmail.com.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
