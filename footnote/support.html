Frequently asked questions

Why can't I generate cards from a PDF?

Generating needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everything else (writing cards, studying, importing, statistics) works without it.

Are my PDFs sent anywhere?

No. Cards, questions and notes are made by the model on your device.

How do my cards get to my other device?

Sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices. Decks, cards and review history sync by themselves. PDF files stay on the device they were added on; add the same PDF on the other device to open its pages there.

What is free and what is Pro?

Writing cards, studying, importing and statistics are free, and so are 50 generations every month. Footnote Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited generation and for saving or printing worksheets.

I bought Pro but it is not active.

Open Settings in the App and choose Restore Purchase, signed in with the Apple account you bought it with.

Can I bring cards from another app?

Yes: import a deck file (.apkg) or a CSV file. You can also export your cards as CSV or as a deck file at any time.

A generated card is wrong.

Generated content can contain mistakes, which is why every candidate waits for your approval. Reject it or edit it before adding.
