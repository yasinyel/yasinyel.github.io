Frequently asked questions

How is the daily number calculated?

Your regular payments, installments and the savings you set aside are subtracted from your income; what's left is divided over the days until payday. On days you spend less, the remainder is added to the following days.

I entered an expense by mistake.

Tap the expense on the Today screen or in History; you can fix the amount or delete it.

Why doesn't Kozu connect to my bank?

On purpose. So that your bank password is never shared with anyone, Kozu connects to no bank; you add expenses yourself, which takes five seconds.

Which month does an installment land in?

It follows your card's statement day. The first installment of a purchase made after the statement day goes on the next statement.

How do I add the home screen widget?

On iPhone, touch and hold the home screen, tap the plus at the top left and choose Kozu. On Android, touch and hold the home screen, open Widgets and choose Kozu.

What happens to my data if I change phones?

Export your data to a file in Settings > Backup, then restore it from the same screen on the new phone.

Is there a free trial?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial. The subscription starts unless you cancel before the trial ends.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions > Kozu. Android: Google Play > your profile picture > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Kozu.
