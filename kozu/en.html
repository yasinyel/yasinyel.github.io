Kozu

How much can I spend today?

For iPhone and Android · 8 languages · Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Kozu shows you one number: what you can spend today and still make it to payday. Enter your pay and your monthly payments once; Kozu does the math.

  • One number a day: Spend less and tomorrow's number grows; spend more and the difference is spread over the days left.
  • Installments in the right month: Enter a purchase once; each installment lands in the right month based on your card's statement date.
  • Look ahead: A calendar of the periods ahead: what comes in, what goes out, what's left for you.
  • Categories and limits: Set an optional limit per category and see how close you are.
  • Share with your partner: Join with an invite link; iPhone and Android work together. Data is encrypted on your phone.
  • Your data stays with you: Never asks for your bank login. No account, no sign-up.
