Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the Kozu app (the "App") handles your data.

1. Who we are

Yasin Yel — yasinyel32@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

The App collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Apart from the shared budget (section 9), the App sends no data to any server.

3. Information stored on your device

Unless you use a shared budget (section 9), the income, regular payments, expenses, installments, categories and settings you enter are stored only on your device and shared with the App's home screen widgets. This information never reaches us.

For cards, only the name you give, the statement day, the due day and an optional limit are stored. The App never asks for or stores a card number, expiry date or security code.

4. Bank information

The App does not connect to any bank. It never asks for your bank password, online banking details or account activity.

5. Backup file

You can export your data to a file from the Backup screen. You decide where to save that file and who to send it to; it never reaches us.

6. Notifications

The morning number, evening reminder and payment reminders are local notifications created on your device. No remote notification server is used and no marketing notifications are sent.

7. Purchases

Kozu Pro subscriptions are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your payment details never reach us.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children. Because no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.

9. Shared budget

The shared budget is optional; if you don't start one, your data never leaves your device. When you start a shared budget or join one with an invite, the budget data is encrypted on your device and stored encrypted on our server (on Cloudflare's infrastructure) so that it can sync between the members' devices. The encryption key exists only on the members' devices and in the invite link and is never sent to the server, so we cannot read the data on the server either. The name you enter for the members list is part of that encrypted data.

Anyone who has the invite link can see and change the budget; send it only to the person you want to share the budget with. We keep no request logs on the server; the infrastructure provider processes your IP address temporarily in order to deliver the request. When you leave a shared budget, a copy stays on your device; the encrypted record on the server remains for the other members. If you want the record deleted, write to us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We hold no data about you. You can remove everything stored on your device from the App's Settings screen or by deleting the App. For any request, write to yasinyel32@gmail.com.

11. Changes

This policy may be updated. Significant changes will be announced on this page.
