Peregrin: Walk & Ride the World

Every walk goes somewhere.

Now on the App Store · Google Play coming soon · 8 languages

Download on the App StoreAndroid: coming soon to Google Play

Peregrin turns your everyday walks, runs and rides into journeys along the world's great routes. At every stop you discover a story, answer a short quiz and collect a souvenir.

  • Eight historic routes: begin free on the road of the first marathon, from Marathon to Athens, then the Lycian Way, the Olympic Torch Road, the Boston Marathon, the Silk Road, the Camino de Santiago, Kumano Kodo and the Spartathlon.
  • Something new every day: rest stops with local facts, a stage badge every week, a medal at the finish: bronze, silver, gold, and the olive wreath.
  • Every kind of movement counts: walking, running and cycling, read from Apple Health or Health Connect, or recorded with Peregrin's own GPS.
  • Stories you can hear: an audio guide narrates every stop in 8 languages.
  • Travel buddies (optional): invite a friend and see each other on the same route.
  • Share what you learned: story cards for Instagram that show more than distance.
  • Private by design: no account, no ads. Your activity, health and location data stay on your phone.
