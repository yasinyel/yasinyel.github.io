Frequently asked questions

My steps are not moving me forward.

Connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android) in Profile > Activity sources, and allow distance and workouts. Peregrin refreshes when you open the app and every 15 minutes while it is open.

Does cycling count?

Yes. Walking, running and cycling all count on every route. For medals, cycling distance counts as one third of the effort of walking or running.

How do I record a walk, run or ride?

Tap Start on the map screen, choose walk, run or ride, and start. Recording continues with the screen locked. On Android you can choose to allow location only while the app is open.

I don't see notifications.

Check Profile > Notifications in the app and allow notifications for Peregrin in your phone's settings. Peregrin sends at most two reminders a day.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Peregrin. Android: Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Peregrin.

How do I delete my data?

All data is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app removes it. You can also tap Start over in Profile.
